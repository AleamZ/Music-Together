# v18.10 Sốc nhiệt & bơi chủ động — plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-v18-design.md` §18.10 (approved: 35°C, 10 min continuously outdoors, shops and the
restaurant are shade). Builds on v18.1 (swim mode, `sw`, `_pond_spot`), v18.3 (vitals, the faint flow) and v18.8
(`room_weather`, `vitals_tick(token, room)`).

## Server — `supabase/migrations/0033_heat_swim.sql` (additive, re-runnable, after 0031; 0032 is independent)
A. `room_weather.temp_c numeric` (null = unknown). `set_room_weather` gains `p_temp_c numeric default null` (validated
   −50..60; the old 8-argument signature is dropped, so older named calls still resolve). `_weather_json` adds `temp_c`.
B. Shade: `_in_shade(p_map, p_x, p_y)`, a literal list of rects per map (the porch of each shop, depot and the
   restaurant). `lib/game/heat/shade.ts` mirrors it and `tests/unit/heat.test.ts` pins the two equal.
C. `heat_state` (one row per account): outdoor_since, shocked, immune_until, warm_started_at, warm_until, swimming,
   swim_room, cramp_until, cramp_room, last_seen.
D. Pure helpers: `_heat_hot(kind, is_day, temp_c)` (clear, day, ≥ 35) and `_cramp_chance(shocked, warmed)`
   (0.10 / 0.005 / 0). Mirrored in `lib/game/heat/model.ts`.
E. `_heat_resolve(account)`: a cramp past its deadline drowns: the v18.3 faint (fainted_until = now + 10 s), the swim ends.
F. `vitals_tick(token, room, p_map, p_x, p_y)` (the new arguments default to null; the 2-argument version is dropped):
   resolves drowning, then accrues heat. The player is outdoors when the position is known, not in shade, not swimming
   and not fainted. With the room hot and no immunity, outdoor_since starts (a heartbeat gap > 120 s restarts it);
   10 min makes the player heat-shocked. Anything else clears both. Thirst drains at the weather rate × 2 while shocked.
   The answer adds `heat` (the state json).
G. RPCs, all members only:
   - `heat_state(room, token)`
   - `warm_up_start(room, token, col, row)` / `warm_up_finish(room, token)`: a pond shore or dock cell, 10 s apart (≥ 9.5 s
     and ≤ 60 s), warm for 5 min.
   - `jump_in(room, token, col, row)`: a shore or dock cell, dry and awake. It rolls the cramp while shocked
     (`_cramp_chance`, random()), then sets swimming, and cramping for 10 s on a failed roll. Shock clears (the water).
   - `leave_water(room, token)`: swimming and not cramping, then 10 min immunity.
   - `rescue_swimmer(room, token, victim, col, row)`: another member of the room cramping in this room. The rescuer's
     cell must be a pond cell (water or a cast cell). The victim leaves the water with immunity.
   - Positions and proximity are client claims, like every position in the game. The server checks the map geometry
     only.

## Client
- `lib/game/heat/{model,shade,rpc}.ts`: constants, `crampChance`, `parseHeat`, chips text, `inShade`, the RPC wrappers
  and error texts.
- `vitals-rpc.ts`: `vitalsTick(token, room, where?)` sends the map and position; `VitalsState.heat`. `useVitals` takes an
  optional `where` getter. `weather/rpc.ts` reports `tempC`.
- Protocol: `hx` on st/mv/pa (a bitmask: 1 shocked, 2 warming up, 4 cramping) and `cr` (the cramp's ms left, 0..10000).
- Engine:
  - `setHeat({ shocked, crampLeftMs })`, `warmUp()` (a 10 s stretch; movement is locked), `jumpIn()` (into the water
    cell the shore faces).
  - `heatProbe()`: the shore cell, swimming, and the nearest cramping member within 28 px.
  - E rescues when a cramping member is in range.
  - Drawing: red face and sweat, the stretch, and a cramping swimmer who sinks and waves with a countdown. Remote
    players are drawn the same way from `hx`/`cr`.
- `components/game/HeatLayer.tsx` (one additive line in GameShell):
  - the HUD chips 🥵 / 🏊 mm:ss / 🧘;
  - the pond-edge buttons "🌊 Nhảy xuống ao" and "🧘 Khởi động";
  - "🛟 Cứu" (E), the cramp countdown and polling, leaving the water, and a vitals reload on drowning (the faint flow).
- Art: `lib/game/art/heat.ts`. Preview `scratchpad/sprites/heat.png`.
