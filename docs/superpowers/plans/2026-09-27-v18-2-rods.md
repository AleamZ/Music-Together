## SQL (0034, additive, re-runnable)

- A. `shop_items.durability` (max; null = unbreakable), `radius_px`, `bite_boost` (default 1); kind check + `net`;
  the rows above (upsert); `inventory.durability` (null = none), back-filled to max for owned rods.
- B. `_rod_wear(account, rod, amount)` fills the 0031 seam: durability −amount (floor 0); at 0 the rod is unequipped
  (profile rod → rod_wood). `_rod_usable(account, rod)`.
- C. `_fishing_state` (0015 body) + `owned` includes nets, + `wear` `{id: [d, max]}` for rods and nets.
- D. `buy_item` (0015 body) + `net` kind; a rod/net is inserted at full durability; a broken rod is "already owned".
  `set_loadout` (0015 body) + a broken rod raises `rod broken`. `repair_rod(token, item)`.
- E. `start_cast` (0031 body) + a broken equipped rod raises `rod broken`; `bite_boost` of the bait.
- F. `finish_cast` (0031 body, 4 args) + wear 1 on every finish except `no_bite` / `overboard` (overboard keeps its 3);
  overboard also grants the v18.10 swim immunity (as `leave_water`: immune 10 min, heat shock cleared);
  answers carry `rod_broke`.
- G. Nets: `net_throws` (one per account; `haul`, `hauled_at`), pure `_net_quality(charge_ms)`,
  `_net_catch(charge_ms, offsets, big)`, `_net_beat_ms(seed)`.
  `start_net(room, token, col, row, net)` — at the release: checks spot, storm, vitals, the cast caps (a throw counts as
  a cast), a free slot; spends one throw (a net at 0 is removed); returns throw id, seed, beat_ms.
  `net_haul(token, throw, charge_ms, offsets)` — the net sank: expired (120 s) / too_early (< 0.9 × 5 beats) lose the
  throw; else rolls `_net_catch` fish (rarity 1–2, room price index, clipped to free room) onto the row (`empty` when 0).
  `finish_net` — re-created by 0037 with the mistake count (see step 5 below; 0034's `won` version is dropped).
  Client trust (documented in the SQL, like v18.1's p_hooked): offsets (which shadows the net covered) and `won` are
  client claims, bounded by the server (≤ 5 common/uncommon fish, the charge ceiling, the free room, the time gates);
  claiming a loss only costs the claimant.

Scoring (mirrored by `lib/game/fishing/net.ts`, pinned by tests): power = (1 − cos 2πt/1200)/2 at release;
base = 2 + round-half-up(3q) (+1 big net), ≤ 5; five fish shadows = the five "beats": a shadow under the net is sent as
offset 0 (hit), one outside as 9999 (miss); each miss −1 fish.

## Minigame (owner's redesign, 2026-09-27)

1. **Aim:** fish shadows swim in the water in front of the player (a seeded school); a ring follows the mouse / arrow
   keys, clamped to the throw range. Bar: "Di chuột chọn chỗ – giữ chuột/Space để lấy đà". Esc / "Thôi" cancels (free).
2. **Power:** holding fills a bar next to the character (swells and falls back, period 1.2 s); its top 15% is green.
   Green = full-size net landing on the aim; less = a smaller ring (40–100%) landing short.
3. **Throw:** the release calls start_net; the net flies in an arc (0.7 s) and opens into a circle.
4. **Sink:** 1.5 s; the shadows under the net are highlighted (caught) and stop; then net_haul (after the server's pace).
5. **Kéo lưới (arrow rounds, replaces the tug — owner's change; migration 0037):** rounds of ↑↓←→ sequences typed with
   the arrow keys / WASD / 4 on-screen buttons (touch) before each round's timer bar runs out. The current key is
   yellow, typed ones green, a wrong key red + shake; "Perfect / Good / Miss" per round; "Sai: x/3". Plan from the haul
   (d = Σ(rarity + kg)): rounds = clamp(round(d/2)+1, 2, 5), keys = clamp(4 + ⌊d/3⌋, 4, 9), timer = clamp(4200 − 80d,
   3000, 4200) ms (1 common fish: 2 × 4 keys, 4.1 s; five 1.5 kg uncommon: 5 × 9, 3 s). Each wrong key = 1 mistake; a
   timed-out round = 1 mistake and the next round. `finish_net(token, throw, mistakes)` (0037, 0 … 4): 0 = all the fish,
   1–3 = one random fish escapes per mistake (server's choice), the 4th = kéo hụt: pulled into the pond (v18.1 swim,
   hunger −10, v18.10 immunity), no fish. Server gate: ≥ 0.8 s per round (`_net_rounds`, mirrored) after the haul.6. **Result card:** the pulled bundle with the fish flapping and the catch listed. Durability shown in the header.
