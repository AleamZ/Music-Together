# v18.6 Thời trang 2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:**
- **Accessories.** Three accessory slots are worn at the same time: `neck` (scarves plus new necklaces), `wrist` (bracelets and a watch) and `hairpin` (clips, bows, headbands). They share one store tab, "💍 Phụ kiện", which replaces "Khăn".
- **Hats.** Ten new hat *shapes*.
- **Salon.** A hair salon at Chợ Lớn sells 10 hair styles and 10 hair colours: cut 300 xu, dye 500 xu, both 700 xu. Hair can no longer be chosen in the character editor, including at creation, where a new character gets a default hair.
- **Underwear.** Top and bottom may be left empty. Nam shows boxer briefs; nữ shows a modest bra and briefs.

**Architecture:**
- Server: `0029_fashion2.sql` makes these changes:
  - new slots `wrist` and `hairpin`, and matching `characters` columns;
  - `top` and `bottom` become nullable;
  - hair values are validated against the new lists;
  - a new `save_character` that never changes hair on an existing row and gives new rows a gender default;
  - the RPC `salon_style`;
  - catalog rows for 15 accessories and 10 hats.
- Client:
  - the `Look` type gains `wrist` and `hairpin`, and `top`/`bottom` become `string | null`;
  - the art gains an underwear palette, accessory layers, new hat layers and new hair layers;
  - the store category "accessory";
  - the editor loses its hair pickers and gains "Không mặc";
  - a salon interactable and `SalonModal` in the market.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 (read `node_modules/next/dist/docs` before framework APIs), React 19 + React Compiler lint, TS strict, Tailwind v4, Supabase SECURITY DEFINER RPCs, Vitest 4 (`cleanup()` in `afterEach`), pnpm, throwaway PG18.

**Spec:** approved in chat on 2026-09-27 as an addendum to `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-v18-design.md` (the user answered "B"). The goal above is the spec.

- **Icons:** a 16×16 icon per accessory, drawn by hand in `ITEM_ICONS` style: a chain with a pendant; a bracelet ring; a watch face; a bow; a headband arc; a star clip; a flower; a hair tie loop.
