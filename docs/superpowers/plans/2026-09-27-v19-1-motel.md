# v19.1 Nhà nghỉ + giấc ngủ: plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-v19-housing-design.md` §v19.1. This work is done in place on main and is not committed.

## Task 1: server, `0039_motel.sql`

- **Rules:**
  - `_motel_price`: night 100, month 2000.
  - `_motel_len`: 24 h and 30 days.
- **Tables:** `motel_stays(account_id pk, plan, until, updated_at)` and `rest_state(account_id pk, slept_day, slept_at, buff_until)`. RLS is on and there are no grants.
- **`_rest_factor(uuid)`:** 0.7 while `buff_until > now()`, else 1.
- **`_motel_json`:** `{stay:{plan,until_ms}|null, rest:{buff_until_ms|null, slept_today}, server_now_ms}`.
- **RPCs** (SECURITY DEFINER, session token):
  - `motel_state`.
  - `motel_rent(plan)`: wallet lock, then extends from `greatest(now, until)`, with a 60-day cap, ledger `motel`, and returns `coins`.
  - `motel_sleep`: needs an active stay, not fainted, and one sleep per Vietnam day; sets `buff_until = now + 24 h`.
- **Ledger:** 0038's 31 reasons plus `motel`.
