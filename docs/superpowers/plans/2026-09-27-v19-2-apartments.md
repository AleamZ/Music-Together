# v19.2 Khu nhà + chung cư + nội thất: plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-v19-housing-design.md` §v19.2 (plus the global rulings R1–R6).
Base: v19.1 (0039, 0040) on main, uncommitted. Work in place on main; no commit.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-27)

- Sleep gives only the buff (no hunger/thirst refill). Motel prices stay.
- **The apartment TV plays the apartment's own YouTube queue**, shared by the owner and the guests inside that apartment
  (it replaces the spec's "mirror the room's queue" ruling). It reuses the app's YouTube player hook, the room's
  clock-based sync (`targetSeconds` / `needsResync`) and the link parser; it is scoped to the apartment, not the room.

## Rulings made in this plan

- **Map.** New `MapId` `khu_nha` ("Khu nhà", 800×400). Portal from Chợ Lớn's east alley (a road trip, like hall ↔ market:
  vehicles matter). The city map shows it at "Khu dân cư" (50, 14); the greyed "sắp mở" place is gone. Streets hold the
  chung cư "Phú Mỹ" (12 units, 3 floors × 4) with its lobby door (interactable `apartment`), 8 fenced lots marked
  "v19.3 sắp mở", the "Sàn bất động sản" office (closed sign, decoration) and a city-map post. No shade porches: khu_nha
  is outdoors everywhere (`_in_shade` lists it as a known map); interiors are not in any list → shaded (R3).
- **Units.** 12. Rent 1500 xu / 30 days (extends from the current end, at most 60 days ahead), buy 25 000 xu (a renter
  may buy their own unit; no refund of the rent). One home per account. Moving out: an owner sells back for 70 %
  (17 500 xu, reason `apartment_sell`); a renter just leaves (no refund). A lapsed rent has a 7-day grace (renew only;
  no entering or sleeping); after it a lazy sweep frees the unit and the furniture goes back to storage (never lost).
- **Access model.** The owner sets the door: `private` (only invited guests), `room` (members of the music room the
  owner is a member of — both must be members of the room passed in) or `open` (anyone). Anyone else **knocks**: the
  knock lands in the owner's list (polled while the owner is inside or at the lobby) and the owner accepts (a 3 h guest
  pass) or refuses. The owner can also revoke a pass. Only the owner edits.
- **Interior.** Grid 14 × 10 tiles of 16 px (224 × 160 world px). Rows 0–1 are the wall. The door is cells (6–7, 9) and
  the cells above it (6–7, 8) stay clear. Items have w × h tiles; rotation 0–3 (odd swaps w/h). Rugs only collide with
  rugs (they lie under furniture); everything else collides with everything but rugs. At most 60 placed items.
  Walls and floors are surfaces: owning one (bought like furniture, not consumed) lets the owner apply it.
- **Interior view.** A full-screen overlay (like the motel's private view, but walkable and shared): its own canvas,
  the normal `stepMove` collision on a grid built from the layout, the local character plus everyone else inside, on
  its own realtime channel `room:<roomId>:int:apt:<no>` (presence: name + look; broadcast: positions, "layout changed",
  "tv changed"). The street map keeps my character at the lobby door (presence map stays `khu_nha`).
- **Furniture store** "Nội thất cô Năm" at Chợ Lớn mid-street, west of the motel: `FURNITURE_FRONT = {x:420, y:208,
  w:72, h:36}`. Catalogue (TS mirror of `furniture_catalog`, pinned by a unit test): beds, tables, chairs, sofas, lamps,
  plants, rugs, shelves, the TV, two fridges, four wallpapers and three floors, in three styles (gỗ, hiện đại, mây tre).
- **Fridge.** `fridge_fish` rows (the fish row moves table, same id/weight/price/caught_at). Capacity = the sum of the
  placed fridges' capacities (small 20, big 50). Putting in needs an active home and space; taking out needs an active
  home and bag space (`1 + _bucket_cap`). Fish in the fridge don't count against the bag; there is no spoilage in the
  game, so the fridge is extra storage. Selling stays bag-only.
- **Sleep at home.** `home_sleep(token, kind, id)`: `motel` delegates to `motel_sleep`; `apt` needs the active unit
  and a placed bed. Same `rest_state` → one sleep per Vietnam day across motel and home.
- **TV.** `apt_tv` (queue jsonb, current, started_at). Anyone inside may add (≤ 30 queued, YouTube id checked); the
  owner or the adder skips; the next track starts when any viewer reports the end (`tv_next` guarded by the current id).
  While the TV panel plays, the room's music is ducked on this device and restored on close.
- **Anti-cheat wipe.** `_ac_wipe` (newest 0019) re-created verbatim plus the `v19.2` lines: fridge fish, furniture
  and the home go too.
- **Ledger.** 0039's 32 reasons + `apartment`, `apartment_sell`, `furniture` = 35.
