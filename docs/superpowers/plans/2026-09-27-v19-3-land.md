# v19.3 Đất + xây nhà + cho thuê phòng: plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-v19-housing-design.md` §v19.3 (plus R1–R6). Base: v19.2 (0041) on main,
uncommitted. Work in place on main; no commit. Patterns: v19.2's plan and report (`.superpowers/sdd/v19-2-apartments-report.md`).

## Owner's brief (2026-09-27)

The 8 fenced lots on Khu nhà, buying land, a tile house builder (walls, doors, windows, floors, roof style, rooms; a cost
per tile; build time optional), the built house on the street for everyone, a walkable interior decorated with v19.2's
furniture, and rooms rented to other players: the owner sets the rent **per 30 days** (replaces the spec's nightly price),
the tenant pays, the owner gets 95 %, 5 % is a fee; the tenant enters, sleeps and decorates **their room only**.

## Rulings

- **Lots.** 8 lots (`LOTS` of `maps/khu-nha.ts`), each a 20 × 14 tile grid. Land costs **40 000 xu** from the city and
  includes the first 30 days of upkeep. **Upkeep 500 xu / 30 days**, extended from the current end, at most 60 days ahead
  (R5). Unpaid upkeep: the lot is *in arrears* (no building, no rent listing, tenants cannot pay). **60 days** after the
  upkeep ran out the city repossesses it: the owner gets **50 % of the land price** (20 000 xu, `house_refund`), the house
  is demolished (the build cost is lost) and every item placed in it goes back to its owner's storage.
  Tenants cannot be left inside: while in arrears nobody can extend, and a tenant pays at most 60 days ahead, so every
  tenancy ends before the repossession; any leftover is refunded pro rata anyway.
- **Giving the lot back.** The owner may sell the lot back to the city for the same 50 % (20 000) at any time when no
  room has an active tenant (`has tenants`). The house goes, the furniture goes to storage.
- **One home per account** (v19.2's rule, widened): an account holds at most one of — an apartment (rented, in grace or
  bought), a lot (with or without a house), a room tenancy. So owning land counts as the home from the purchase (one lot
  per account, no land hoarding), and a tenant cannot also own or rent elsewhere. `apt_rent` / `apt_buy` are re-created
  from 0041 with the widened check (`_home_other`).
- **The builder.** Cells: `.` yard, `f` floor, `w` wall, `d` door, `n` window. Prices: floor 10, wall 20, door 150,
  window 100 xu. Saving a design pays for every cell that changes to a non-yard type (its new type's price); clearing a
  cell is free and never refunded. `build_cost` accumulates (v19.4's appraisal). **No build time**: a save is the house
  (the owner's "optional"; a scaffold timer would add a state to every RPC for little play).
  A design is valid when:
  1. it has 280 cells of `.fwdn` (or is all yard: bare land);
  2. every floor cell is enclosed: its 4 neighbours are inside the lot and not yard;
  3. a door has one axis whose two sides are open (floor, or yard for a front door) with at least one floor side, and
     the other axis's two sides are wall, window or door; a front door opens onto a yard cell inside the lot;
  4. a window touches the outside (a yard cell or the lot's edge);
  5. rooms are the 4-connected floor areas: 1–6 rooms, each at least 4 cells, numbered by their first cell (row-major);
  6. at least one front door, and every room reachable from a front door through doors.
  The same checks run in TS (`checkDesign`) and SQL (`_house_check`). Roof styles: `ngoi` (ngói đỏ), `tole` (tôn xanh),
  `la` (lá dừa), `bang` (mái bằng): free to change. The grid cannot change while a room has an active tenant
  (`has tenants`; the roof can); a grid change drops the room listings and returns items no longer on a valid floor to
  their owners' storage.
- **The street.** Every lot shows its house for everyone: the exterior is generated from the grid (4 px per cell, the
  roof style over the built cells, windows and front doors on the outer walls), drawn by the engine over the lot's
  background (`setHouses`), refreshed every 20 s while on Khu nhà. Each lot's gate is an interactable `lot`.
- **Interior.** A walkable view of the whole lot (20 × 14 tiles of 16 px): yard, floors and doors are walkable; walls,
  windows and furniture (not rugs) are solid. One enters on the yard cell outside the first front door. Presence on
  `room:<roomId>:int:house:<lot>`. Who may enter: the owner, the active tenants, and whoever the owner's door setting
  lets in (private / room members / open, as v19.2; no knocking in houses this version).
- **Furniture.** v19.2's catalogue and storage. `furniture_items.lot_no` marks an item placed in a house (x, y, rot on
  the lot grid). An item fits when its footprint is on floor cells of one room, not on the floor cell just inside a
  door, and overlaps nothing (rugs only collide with rugs); at most 80 items per house. The owner places in any room
  without an active tenant; a tenant places only in their room; each picks up only their own items (the owner's items
  in a rented room stay while it is rented: "kèm phòng"). Walls and floors: the owner applies a wallpaper and a floor
  surface (owned, as v19.2) to the whole house.
- **Renting rooms.** The owner lists a room at **300–5 000 xu per 30 days** (or unlists it). A tenant pays 30 days,
  extended from the current end, at most 60 days ahead; one tenant per room, one tenancy per account (so one per
  house). The tenant's xu: 95 % to the owner (`house_rent_income`), 5 % is burned (`house_rent_pay` is the full price).
  Both wallets are locked in account-id order. Eviction: the owner unlists the room; the tenant stays until the paid
  period ends (not before). A tenancy ends when its time runs out (lazy sweep, no grace): the tenant's items in the
  house go back to their storage. A tenant may leave early (no refund).
- **Sleep.** `home_sleep(token, 'house', lot)`: the owner needs a bed in a room that is not rented out; a tenant needs a
  bed in their room. One sleep per Vietnam day, shared with the motel and the apartment.
- **Fridge.** A fridge placed in my home counts: `_fridge_cap` sums my placed fridges in my apartment or in the house
  where I own the lot or rent a room; `fish_move_to_*` need any home (`_home_need`). **TV:** apartments only this
  version; in a house it is a decoration (a concern for v19.4).
- **Anti-cheat wipe.** `_ac_wipe` (0041) + `v19.3` lines: the wiped account's tenancy ends; its lot is freed (no refund
  to the wiped account; its tenants are refunded pro rata); its items anywhere go.
- **Ledger.** 0041's 35 reasons + `house_land`, `house_upkeep`, `house_build`, `house_refund`, `house_rent_pay`,
  `house_rent_income` = 41.
- **v19.2 fix.** The TV's duck persists a "ducked from X" marker (`localStorage`); if the tab closes while ducked, the
  next load restores the room volume to X.
