# v19.4 Sàn bất động sản: plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-v19-housing-design.md` §19.4 (and R4–R6). Builds on v19.2 (flats, 0041) and
v19.3 (lots and houses, 0042). Work on main, uncommitted, TDD.

## Rulings

- **The office.** Khu nhà's "SAN BDS" office (`ESTATE_OFFICE`) opens: the shutter becomes a glass door, the "SẮP MỞ" board
  goes; an interactable `estate` at the door (use (648, 176), facing up) and a broker NPC, "anh Tư môi giới", at (692, 156).
  GameShell `case "estate"` opens `EstateModal` (the office's interior painted as its header: counter, broker, listings board).
- **What can be sold.** A bought flat (`tenure = 'own'`; a rented one cannot), or my lot (bare land, or the house with its
  land). The listing is per account (one home per account, so at most one listing per seller).
- **Appraisal** (server-side, recomputed when listing and again when buying):
  - flat: 25 000 (the list price);
  - lot: 40 000 (the land) + 80 % of `build_cost` (what the builder was paid);
  - plus, when listed "kèm nội thất", the catalogue price of the seller's own items placed in it (tenants' items never count).
- **Price band:** `ceil(appraisal × 50 %)` … `appraisal × 300 %`. When buying, a price outside the band of the current
  appraisal (the seller picked up the furniture or rebuilt after listing) refuses `listing stale` and drops the listing.
- **Fee:** the buyer pays the price (`estate_buy`); the seller gets 95 % (floor) (`estate_sale`); the 5 % is burned.
- **Listing life:** expires after 14 days. A 24 h cooldown before listing again applies after a cancel, after an expiry,
  and to a buyer after a purchase (no instant flip). No price edits: cancel and relist (the cooldown applies).
- **Blocked:** a lot in arrears (`upkeep due`), a rented flat, buying my own listing (`own listing`), a buyer with any home
  (flat of any tenure incl. grace, lot, or a room tenancy → `already have a home`), a stale listing (the seller no longer
  owns the property: moved out, gave the lot back, repossessed; the sweep drops those), a price mismatch with what the
  buyer saw (`price changed`).
- **Round trips:** two accounts that traded a property (either direction) within 7 days cannot trade again
  (`suspicious trade`). A sale between two accounts that traded within 30 days is recorded `flagged`; the admin's
  anti-cheat tab lists flagged sales of the last 90 days (`admin_estate_flags`).
- **Title moves atomically** (listing row, both wallets in account order, then the property row, all `for update`).
  - Flat: owner → buyer, `since` = now, door → private; knocks, guest passes and the TV queue are cleared.
  - Lot: owner → buyer, door → private; the upkeep's `paid_until` carries over; **tenancies and room listings stay** (the
    tenants' paid periods are honoured; later rent goes to the new owner).
  - Furniture: "kèm nội thất" → the seller's items placed there change `account_id` to the buyer; otherwise they go to the
    seller's storage. Tenants' items are untouched. Wall/floor surfaces stay only with "kèm nội thất" (else reset).
  - The house stays with the land (the design, rooms, roof and `build_cost`).
- **History:** every sale is kept (`estate_sales`: kind, number, price, fee, kèm nội thất, names at the time, flagged).
  The panel shows the last 30 sales and, per property, its history.
- **News:** a sale of ≥ 50 000 xu writes a Báo Làng line (kind `house`) in the buyer's last room.
- **Wipe:** `_ac_wipe` (from 0042) + one v19.4 line: the wiped account's listing and cooldown go (before `_house_wipe`).
- **Ledger:** 0042's 41 reasons + `estate_sale`, `estate_buy`: 43.
