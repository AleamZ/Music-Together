# v20.3 plan: Bãi đất trống — rings, challenge and stakes, rollback netcode, streamed PvP settlement

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-v20-fight-design.md` (§v20.3, global rulings R1–R9, owner rulings at the end:
keep the realtime caps 2 live fights per room and 4 overall with 10 Hz batching; a 5 % burned fee on PvP stakes).
Builds on v20.1 / v20.2 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-28-v20-1-engine.md`, `…-v20-2-dojo.md`, their reports in
`.superpowers/sdd/`). Branch `feat/v20-fight`. Migration `0051_bai_dat.sql` (0055 is an unrelated blacklist migration and
stays untouched; 0052 is reserved for v20.4).

Also in scope, two v20.2 follow-ups: `_ac_wipe` clears the fight and martial-arts tables, and the world chibi's uniform
shows the wearer's current belt colour.

## Rulings made in this plan

- **P1: the fee.** The pot is `2 × stake`; the fee is `pot × fee_pct / 100` (integer, `fight_config.fee_pct = 5`) and is
  burned; the winner is credited `pot − fee` (`fight_win`). A draw, a void and a conflict refund each stake
  (`fight_refund`) with no fee. Stakes are debited at match start (`fight_stake`). The presets are multiples of 100, so
  the fee is exact.
- **P2: bystanders read `ring_state`, not presence.** The ring label ("⚔️ Hiệp 2 · 1–0", "Góc Đỏ: Lan · chờ đối thủ")
  is drawn by the world engine over each ring from `ring_state`, which bai_dat clients already fetch (on entry, 150 ms
  after an `rg` hint, every 5 s while a ring is occupied). No presence field is added, so the presence budget is
  untouched. The fighters' chibis stay at their corners.
- **P3: the ring hint.** `rg = {id, r, v}` on the map's game channel (`game:{room}:bai_dat`), budget `rg` 2 a second,
  burst 4, per sender. It is sent after every successful `ring_*` call; it carries nothing a client trusts.
- **P4: the stake lock.** `pvp_locked_until` (the 2-conflicts rule) refuses only staked offers and accepts (`'pvp
  locked'`); taking a corner and friendly fights stay open, as the spec's lock text says.
- **P5: the corner hold.** A corner held 5 minutes with the other corner empty is released lazily; the offer lapses 30 s
  after it was made; any new offer (or a corner change) resets both acceptances.
- **P6: refusals at match start are answers.** When both have accepted, `ring_accept` re-checks both fighters
  (uniform, vitals, lock, balances) and the caps; a refusal answers `{refused: code, who}` with the acceptances cleared
  and nothing moved (codes: `not enough xu`, `ring busy`, `daily fight limit`, `no uniform`, `too hungry to fight`,
  `too thirsty to fight`, `pvp locked`). Offer races answer `{refused: 'offer changed'}`.
- **P7: the caps** are counted from `fight_matches` (kind `pvp`, created on the current VN day): live per room and
  overall, staked per account, staked and friendly per pair. Voided and disputed matches count.
- **P8: match params.** 3 rounds (best of 3), maxRounds 5, a fresh seed, `delay` = the larger proposed N (2–6), p1 = the
  red corner, p2 = the blue corner, each with the worn uniform's style at its rank's unlocks (`_fx_style_of`). Frame 0 is
  `now() + 3 s`.
- **P9: frames and delay.** Frame k's inputs are `a[k], b[k]`; the local key mask sampled while the local sim steps frame
  t is the input of frame `t + N` (frames `0 … N − 1` are 0). A client's own inputs are final once generated and are
  never replaced (pushed or not).
- **P10: rollback by re-simulation.** The session keeps the confirmed state `C` at frame `c` (every input of frames
  `< c` known) and re-simulates the prediction `c → f` from it whenever the displayed frame is needed (≤ W = 12 frames of
  steps). The prediction repeats the opponent's last confirmed mask. Rollback depth = `f − c`. If `f − (remoteAck + 1) ≥
  W` the local frame stalls. The HUD banners, the KO letters, the end of the match and the result come from `C` only.
- **P11: time sync.** The local frame target is the server-clock frame minus a slip. The opponent's frame is estimated
  from its newest input frame (`− N + 1`) plus the time since that packet arrived plus half the RTT. When the local
  frame is more than 2 frames ahead, the slip grows by 1 every 20 frames; when it is behind by more than 2, the slip
  shrinks (never below 0).
- **P12: packets.** `fi {f, r, a, h?}` every 6 frames, or early on an attack press edge with ≥ 3 frames since the last
  send, at most 12 a second; `r` covers own frames `ack + 1 …` newest, cut to 200 runs; `h = [frame, hash]` for the
  newest multiple of 60 the sender has confirmed, sent once per checkpoint. `fp {n, t}` / `fq {n, t}` ping-pong on the
  ready screen (10 pings, 2 a second). `fr {f}`: "I resynced at frame f". Every packet carries the sender id; packets
  from anyone but the opponent are dropped; budgets `FIGHT_LIMITS` (`fi` 15/20, `fp` 2/6, `fq` 2/6, `fr` 1/3).
- **P13: RTT and N.** `rttP90` from the ready screen's pongs, then from acks during play (the send time of the newest
  frame a packet carried vs the arrival of an ack that covers it). `L = ceil((rttP90 / 2 + 100) / 16.67)`,
  `N = clamp(ceil(L / 2), 2, 6)`; L > 18 refuses ("Mạng hai bên chậm quá để đấu (≈ X ms)").
- **P14: pushes in PvP.** Every 60 frames: own runs `[pushed, to)` (≤ 300), `seen` = the opponent's inputs received
  contiguously `[seenPushed, remoteAck]` (≤ 300), and the hash of the newest confirmed checkpoint. During a stall an
  empty keepalive every 3 s. At the confirmed end the rest is pushed at once.
- **P15: the server's hash check in PvP is deferred.** A client's `(frame, hash)` is kept as that side's pending hash
  (`fight_logs.pend_hash_frame / pend_hash`) and compared when the server's sim passes that frame, provided that side's
  `seen` has been checked up to it (so a conflict is never mistaken for a modified sim). A mismatch soft-flags
  `fight_hash_mismatch` once per match and side, marks that side `resync` (answered on its next push or state), and turns
  hard when the account has mismatches in 3 matches within 7 days.
- **P16: the conflict check.** Each side's `seen` log is stored like its runs (`seen_runs`, `seen_frontier`) and
  compared with the other side's canonical runs over the frames both cover, from `seen_checked + 1`, at most 1 200
  frames per call. The first difference halts the match: status `disputed`, end reason `conflict`, both stakes refunded,
  no vitals cost, a `fight_conflicts` row (reporter = the side whose seen differs, reported = the other), soft
  `fight_log_conflict` to both. Per account: 2 conflicts in 30 days set `pvp_locked_until = now() + 7 days`; 3 with at
  least 2 distinct opponents flag hard `fight_log_conflict_pattern`.
- **P17: absence.** `fight_claim` wins when the opponent's `last_push_at` (else `started_at`) is older than 20 s and mine
  is within 10 s: `timeout_claim`, a loss for the absentee whatever the score. Too early is an answer (`{claimed: false,
  wait_ms}`). Both absent 120 s voids (`abandon`, refunds, soft `fight_abandon` to both). Overtime: 20 minutes after
  frame 0 the match is decided from the server's current sim (it trails the slower pusher by about a second): the
  round in progress goes to the higher HP‰ (equal: a draw round), the match to the one with more round wins, a tie is a
  draw with refunds (`overtime`). Both are lazy: `ring_state`, every `ring_*`, `fight_push`, `fight_state`,
  `fight_claim` sweep the room's (or the match's) live PvP matches, with `for update skip locked`.
- **P18: stall blame.** At settlement, a side the opponent reports as having caused more than 20 % of the match's
  frames of stall is marked `blamed`; soft `fight_stall_blame` when an account is blamed in 3 matches against at least 2
  opponents within 7 days.
- **P19: lock order.** Match row → ring row → wallets in account-id order, everywhere. Sweeps run before a `ring_*` call
  locks its ring. A ring's live match is read without a lock.
- **P20: reload.** The client writes its own input log to `localStorage` (key `fight:{match}`) before each packet send,
  so the inputs it broadcast survive a reload; on reload it takes the longer of that log and the server's runs (they must
  agree on the common frames). Without storage an honest reload can end in a conflict void (refunds) — accepted.
- **P21: settlement extras.** `_fx_settle` (0049's body plus one marked line) calls `_pvp_settle` for kind `pvp`: pays,
  updates `fight_profiles` (wins, losses, draws), frees the ring's match (both stay in their corners), writes Tin làng
  for a stake ≥ 5 000 ("⚔️ Trận thư hùng ở Bãi đất trống: …").
- **P22: `_ac_wipe`** (0043's body, the newest) first settles the account's live matches as its forfeits
  (`_fx_forfeit_all`, so the opponent is paid), then deletes its corners, exams, enrollments, fight profile and uniforms
  (a worn uniform is taken off). Match rows and logs stay (evidence, pruned by retention). Account deletion runs
  `_fx_forfeit_all` from a new `fight_accounts_bd` trigger.
- **P23: the belt on the world chibi.** `characters.belt smallint` = the rank of the style whose uniform is worn (null
  otherwise), kept by triggers (characters before insert or update of `outfit`; `martial_enrollments` after insert,
  update or delete). `Look.belt` is read with the look; `resolveWear` draws the uniform's belt code in
  `beltColor(style, rank)`; the look key includes it.
- **P24: admin.** `admin_fight_list(token)` (root: the latest disputed, void and conflicted matches with their
  conflicts) and `admin_fight_log(token, match)` (params, both logs, both seen logs, status, result, conflicts). The
  anti-cheat tab gains "⚔️ Trận PvP" with "Xem lại trận": the overlay replays the two logs read-only.
- **P25: bystanders never join the fight topic.** Only the two fighters subscribe to `fight:{room}:r{ring}`.
