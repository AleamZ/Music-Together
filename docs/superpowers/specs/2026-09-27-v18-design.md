# v18: sống ở ao, đồ có độ bền, đói/khát, Chợ Lớn

Approved in chat on 2026-09-27. There are four parts, each with its own plan, migration and trial. Build order: **18.3 → 18.4 → 18.1 → 18.2**.

## Global constraints
- Server-authoritative. Every roll, hunger value, durability value and price is computed in SECURITY DEFINER RPCs, and the client only draws. New RPCs follow the existing custom-session pattern (`p_session_token`) and the anti-cheat guards (0015).
- Migrations are numbered 0025 onward, one or more per part. The owner runs them on Supabase; we only replay them on a throwaway local PG.
- Original art only. No copied assets. No literal invisible characters. UI copy is in Vietnamese.
- Reduced motion: every new animation has a still fallback.
- Nothing is committed or pushed without the owner's explicit request.

## 18.3 Đói và khát

**State:** each account has `hunger` and `thirst`, from 0 to 100 (100 = full). Both are stored server-side with `updated_at`, a `zero_since` timestamp (null unless one of them is 0), and the time the account was last seen online.

**Drain (online only):** the drain runs only while the player is online, which is decided by the existing presence/heartbeat.
- Hunger goes from 100 to 0 in 24 h online, so it drops by 100/24 per hour.
- Thirst goes from 100 to 0 in 16 h online.

The server applies the drain lazily on every read or heartbeat: `elapsed_online × rate`. Time offline does not count; the gap between heartbeats is capped at 2 min.

**Thresholds:**
- Below 25: HUD warning (🍚 / 💧 turn red and pulse).
- At 0 (either one): movement speed ×0.6, and casting, netting and farm actions are refused (`'too hungry'` / `'too thirsty'`, shown in Vietnamese).
- 0 for 10 minutes online in a row: **ngất**.
  - The server sets `fainted_until = now + 10 s` and moves the player to the hall spawn.
  - The client shows a dark overlay with "Bạn đã ngất… hồi sinh sau N giây" and blocks input until the timer ends.
  - On revive, hunger and thirst are set to 30 each. Nothing else is lost.

**HUD:** two small bars (🍚 đói, 💧 khát) next to the coin display, and a tooltip with the exact numbers.

**Tests:** drain math (online only, gap cap); the thresholds; the faint → revive values; speed ×0.6 applied client-side from the server state; actions refused at 0.

## 18.4 Chợ Lớn

**Map:** a new map `market` ("Chợ Lớn"), reached through a portal from the hall; a portal leads back.
- Layout: a street with stalls, a **nhà hàng** (restaurant) and a **tiệm quần áo** (clothing store).
- Decorative stalls with NPCs (fruit, flowers, lanterns); they cannot be interacted with.
- Original pixel art in the style of the existing maps. The map is registered in `maps/registry.ts`, with arrivals and a minimap.

**Tiệm quần áo:**
- The fashion store opens only from the store's interaction spot, with an NPC shopkeeper.
- The fashion-store button in the HUD is removed.
- The wardrobe (Tủ đồ, equipping owned items) stays available everywhere.

**Nhà hàng:**
- There is a cook NPC at the counter, and the interaction opens a menu modal with an Ăn (food) tab and an Uống (drink) tab.
- Fixed menu, roughly 6 foods and 5 drinks. Each item has a price and a restore value: food restores hunger, drink restores thirst, and some items restore a little of both. Values are capped at 100.
- Example items: cơm tấm, phở, bánh mì, bún bò; trà đá, nước mía, cà phê sữa đá, nước dừa.
- 🐟 dishes (cá kho tộ, canh chua cá, cá chiên): the player may hand in **one fish from the bag** for a discount.
  - The discount is 20–80% and scales with the fish's rarity and weight, following a formula shared by the server and the client label.
  - The fish is consumed.
  - Without a fish the dish is bought at full price.
- RPC `eat_meal(p_item, p_fish_id default null)`: it checks the coins and the fish ownership, charges the price, removes the fish, restores the values and returns the new state.

**Tests:** the discount formula and its bounds; the menu data; the portal and map registration; the store only opens from the spot.

## 18.1 Ao sống động

**Cá nhảy (cosmetic, client-only):**
- Every 3–8 s a random water tile on the pond map shows a fish leaping.
- The leap is an arc of about 600 ms, with a splash and a ring on entry and on exit.
- Each client sees its own leaps; nothing is networked.

**Câu gần bờ:**
- A cast is allowed from any walkable tile next to water, not only from the docks.
- The server knows whether the cast is from a dock or from the shore (the tile type is sent and validated against the map data).
- From the shore, the bite chance is ×0.4 and the wait is ×1.5. Rare fish can still bite, at the same relative odds.

**Cá lớn lôi xuống ao:**
- A hooked fish is "lớn" if its rarity is at least rare, or its weight is above the rod's rating.
- If reeling a lớn fish fails, the server marks the player **overboard**:
  - the fish is lost;
  - the rod loses 3 durability;
  - hunger drops by 10;
  - a non-default rod has a 10% chance to be **lost** (removed from the bag and unequipped).
- The player is placed on the nearest water tile, in swim mode:
  - speed ×0.5;
  - a swim sprite (the body sunk to the chest, arms stroking, bubbles, a wake);
  - no actions;
  - stepping onto a shore tile ends swim mode.
- Afterwards the player is "ướt" for 4 s: water drips from the sprite.
- Other players see the swim mode through the existing presence state (a new movement flag).

**Tests:** shore vs dock odds; the overboard outcome table (durability, hunger, the 10% loss via a seeded roll); swim-mode movement rules.

## 18.2 Đồ câu có độ bền, lưới cá

**Rod durability:**
- Every owned rod except the default `rod_wood` has `durability` / `max_durability`.
- Each hook attempt (hit or miss) costs 1; an overboard costs 3.
- At 0 the rod stays in the bag but cannot be equipped or used until it is repaired.
- Chú Tư's shop gets a **Sửa cần** action: it restores durability to max for 30% of the rod's price.
- The durability shows as a small bar on the item card.

**Nets:**
- New shop kind `net`: `net_small` (20 throws) and `net_big` (30 throws, larger radius).
- A net is used up (removed) at 0.
- A throw catches 2–5 fish, from the common and uncommon pool only; the count depends on the minigame score.
- The minigame:
  1. **Gồng lực:** hold Space; a ring pulses between small and large. Release: the quality depends on how close the ring was to its maximum.
  2. **Kéo lưới:** 5 beats at a rhythm; press Space on each beat. Each miss lets 1 fish escape.
- The server rolls the catch from the seed and scores the submitted timings, reusing the reel anti-cheat pattern: the server gives the seed, and the client submits the timing offsets.
- Net art: a thrown net that spreads into a circle on the water, then a pulled bundle with fish flapping in it.

**Chú Tư's new stock:**
- Nets: `net_small`, `net_big`.
- A mid-tier rod `rod_fiber` between bamboo and carbon.
- A top rod `rod_master`, which has the highest weight rating and the most durability.
- A premium bait `bait_gold`, which raises the bite chance for a set number of casts.
- Exact prices are set in the plan against the current economy.

**Tests:** durability decrement and the equip block at 0; the repair price; net scoring → catch count; net use-up; the new catalog rows.

- Sneezing and a pale face.

**Still wet 5 more minutes while cảm lạnh → ngất:** the same faint flow as v18.3 (hall, 10 s revive).

## 18.10 Sốc nhiệt & bơi chủ động (addendum, approved 2026-09-27: 35°C, 10 min outdoors; shade = shops/restaurant)

**Heat shock (sốc nhiệt):**
- **Trigger:** room weather clear, day, temperature ≥ 35°C, and outdoors continuously for 10 min.
  - This needs `temp_c` stored in `room_weather`: the owner client reports it.
- **Effect:** thirst drain ×2; red face and sweat.

**Voluntary swim:**
- A "Nhảy xuống ao" action at the pond's edge enters swim mode (18.1 mechanics).
- Stepping onto the shore gives **10 min immunity** to heat shock.

**Warm-up (khởi động):** a 10 s stretch animation on the shore; it lasts 5 min.

**Cramp (chuột rút):**
- **Chance:** jumping in while heat-shocked gives a 10% chance of a cramp, or 0.5% if warmed up.
- **Cramp state:** the swimmer struggles and sinks, and a 10 s countdown is shown.
- **Rescue:** any other player nearby can press E "Cứu" to rescue them to the shore safely.
- **No rescue:** the swimmer drowns, which uses the v18.3 faint flow (sent to the hall, 10 s revive).

**Authority:** all rolls and states are server-side.

**Lightning strike (sét đánh), added 2026-09-27 as part of 18.9:**
- **Chance:** during rain, thunder or storm, every player outdoors has a 1% chance per minute, rolled server-side in the vitals heartbeat and pro-rated by the credited seconds. Indoors or in shade (shops, restaurant) is safe, and an umbrella does not protect.
- **Hit:** a bolt strikes the player's spot, the character is charred with hair on end, then the v18.3 faint flow runs (hall, 10 s revive).
- **Cooldown:** 30 min per player after a hit.

## 18.13 Đi nhờ xe (addendum, owner request 2026-09-27)

A rider's vehicle takes **one passenger**. A player on foot within ~3 cells (24 px) of a riding member who carries nobody sees **"E · Xin đi nhờ {tên}"** (button + E when no map prompt is shown). The request goes over the map's game channel; the driver sees **"{tên} muốn đi nhờ — Đồng ý / Từ chối"** for 15 s (then it lapses, and the asker's wait lapses too). One prompt at a time: a second request while one is shown is declined at once.

**Accepted:** the passenger's position is the driver's (every client pins it to the driver's actor), the passenger's own movement, taps and every interaction are off; the only control is **"Xuống xe"** (button or E), usable any time. The driver gets **"Cho xuống"**; the driver dismounting (R, a faint, starving, the car left at the pond) also drops the passenger. Dropping sets the passenger down beside the vehicle (the first open spot to the side, then behind, else where they are).

**Art:** bike — sitting side-saddle on the rear rack, legs dangling; moto — on the pillion behind the driver, feet on the rear pegs; car — the other seat, visible through the window like the driver. Seated with the shared `seatedRider` helpers so skirts, trousers and sleeves keep their colours; all four facings; everyone sees both.

**Wire (client only, no migration):** st/mv/pa carry `p` = my passenger (driver) or `lf` = my driver (passenger). A pair is drawn together only while both agree. New control messages `rq` (ask), `ra` (answer, `ok`, the vehicle), `rx` (end, either side) and `lg` (the driver takes a portal to map `m`). Budget: 1 a second, burst 4 per sender. A pair with no agreement 3 s after linking, a driver seen on foot, a driver's `bye` or a partner unseen for 15 s ends the lift.

**Maps:** the driver taking a portal sends `lg`; the passenger follows through the same portal of its current map (a road trip plays the same cutscene with both on the vehicle, the passenger's without the skip button; a driver's paid skip only shortens the driver's own trip). Both keep the lift across the new world (set after the channel exists).

**Refused to ask:** while riding, fishing, swimming, warming/cramping, fainted, on the road, busy with farm work or a card seat, or with any panel open.

## 18.14 Faint ladder (addendum, owner rule 2026-09-28)

"Trong 1 ngày, chết lần đầu 10s, lần 2 5 phút, lần 3 15 phút, lần 4 1 tiếng, lần 5 chết luôn cả ngày." Per Vietnam calendar day (`_vn_today()`, the same day as the one-sleep rule), the Nth faint of any cause (starvation, drowning, the cold faint, lightning) lasts **10 s, 5 min, 15 min, 1 h**; the **5th** lasts until the next VN midnight and locks game mode ("exhausted") until then. The revive (30 hunger / 30 thirst) is unchanged.

**Server (`0045_faint_ladder.sql`):** `vitals.faint_day`, `vitals.faint_count`; every faint goes through `_faint(account)` (counts, returns the length). `_vitals_json` adds `faint_count`, `next_faint_s` and `locked_until_ms` (VN midnight while count ≥ 5). `_vitals_guard` calls `_not_exhausted` first, so every vitals-guarded gameplay RPC refuses with `exhausted`. A drowning while already fainted counts nothing.

**Client:** the faint screen counts down (m:ss / h:mm:ss) and says "Lần ngất thứ N hôm nay · Lần sau: …"; the HUD shows 😵N/5 once N ≥ 1. On the 5th, game mode closes to the classic view (the normal switch, not the crash path) and re-entry shows "Bạn đã kiệt sức 5 lần hôm nay — nghỉ ngơi, mai quay lại nhé. Vẫn nghe nhạc được." with a countdown to midnight. Music, chat and the queue stay open.
