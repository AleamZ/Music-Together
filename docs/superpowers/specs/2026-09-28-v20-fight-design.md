# v20 Võ đài (fighting): design

Date: 2026-09-28. Status: design, not started. Each sub-version gets its own plan in `docs/superpowers/plans/` before code;
rulings made there are recorded at the top of this file, as v19 does.

## Owner's request (summary)

- A fighting area, **Bãi đất trống**: a new map next to Chợ Lớn. Two players step in, a ready screen pops up, then a
  fighting-game screen opens: 2D side view in the Street Fighter mould.
  - Moves: walk, jump, crouch, block, light and heavy punches and kicks, special skills per martial art, key combos.
  - HUD: a health bar and an energy (nội lực) bar.
  - Matches are best of 3 rounds, 99 s each.
- **Bets.** Both players agree a stake in xu, and the fight starts only after both accept.
  - Losing costs only the stake, with no faint, plus a small hunger and thirst cost.
- **PvP sync.** The clients exchange inputs over realtime for smooth play. When the fight ends, the **server** replays the
  whole input log deterministically, as the reel replay does since 0046: a TS and SQL mirror with shared fixtures. The
  replay decides the winner and settles the escrowed stake.
- **Martial arts.** To fight you first learn a style at a **Võ đường** in Chợ Lớn:
  - Vovinam, Muay Thai, Karate, Taekwondo, plus two or three more, each with its own skills and stats.
  - Each style has its own uniform (võ phục). You must wear it to fight, and it replaces top and bottom.
  - Learning costs tuition. Belt exams unlock higher skills. An exam is a kata rhythm minigame **and** a sparring match
    against the master's bot, which gets stronger each belt.
- **Underground tournaments.** A hidden area in Chợ Lớn (**khu bí mật**) hosts **giải ngầm** for prize money:
  - PvP or against bots, by underground rank;
  - a ladder, a rating, boss bots, entry fees, prize pools, daily limits, and anti-farming between friends.
- **Art.** Original pixel art. The fight screen is an overlay with larger fighters, per-style move animations, hit sparks
  and a KO screen.

## Goals and non-goals

**Goals**
- A small, deterministic fighting engine (60 Hz, integer math, no floats, no `Math.random`). It is written once in TS and
  mirrored in plpgsql, and fixtures pin the two together.
- Every fight that moves xu, a belt or a rating is refereed by the server's replay of the players' committed inputs. A
  client's word alone decides nothing that pays.
- Netcode that feels responsive over Supabase Broadcast within the free plan's message budget.
- Seven styles with distinct stats and specials, belts that unlock them, and exams that cost time and xu.
- Stakes held in escrow with clear forfeit rules. Nobody can dodge a loss by disconnecting.

**Non-goals (v20)**
- Projectiles, juggles, air throws, tag teams, more than two fighters, and weapons.
- Betting by spectators. Only the two fighters stake xu.
- P2P WebRTC transport. The packet format keeps a transport seam (`FightTransport`) so it can be added later.
- Sound effects. The room's music keeps playing.
- Cross-room matchmaking. Matches pair players present in the same music room; ratings and belts are per account.
- Mid-fight chat or emotes inside the overlay.

## Global rulings (every sub-version)

- **R1: One engine, two mirrors.** `lib/game/fight/engine.ts` is the source of truth. The plpgsql mirror (`_fx_*`) is
  changed in the same commit as the TS.
  - Every number stays an integer below 2^31, except the u32 PRNG and hash, which are handled as in `_reel_imul`.
  - The fixtures `tests/fixtures/fight-cases.json`, `fight-bot-cases.json` and `kata-cases.json` are generated by
    `scripts/gen-fight-fixtures.ts` from the TS. Both Vitest and the SQL smoke check them.
  - The move and style tables are flattened into one `int[]` literal. A unit test pins the SQL literal against the TS
    table, the same way prices are pinned (CRLF-insensitive).
- **R2: The input is the only thing a client sends to a refereed fight.**
  - Each frame's input is a 10-bit mask. The server never accepts one player's claim about the other player's inputs as
    canonical.
  - Motion detection (quarter circles and so on), cancels and buffering all live inside the sim, so the server replays
    raw buttons.
- **R3: Streamed replay.** The server does not replay a whole match in one call; the anon role's statement timeout is
  about 3 s. Instead:
  - Each `fight_push` (about once a second per player) advances the server's copy of the sim over the frames both logs
    now cover, at most 300 frames per call.
  - The state is stored as `int[]` on the match row.
  - The call that reaches the match's end settles it in the same transaction.
- **R4: Refereed means paid-for.** Practice against a local bot is client-only and pays nothing. Every fight with a
  stake, a belt, a rating or a prize is a `fight_matches` row driven by `fight_push`.
- **R5: Money.**
  - PvP stakes are zero-sum with no house cut: the winner gets exactly the loser's stake. This follows the v16 play-money
    line in the README ("the tables take no cut").
  - Bot-ladder prizes are minted rewards. They are capped by first-clear-per-season rules (§v20.4).
  - Every xu move is a SECURITY DEFINER RPC with `p_session_token`, and it locks wallets in account-id order.
  - Each migration re-creates `coin_ledger_reason_check` from the newest full list (0043's 43 reasons) plus its own
    reasons.
- **R6: Vitals.**
  - A fight never faints anyone.
  - At settlement each fighter loses **2 hunger and 3 thirst per round played**. This applies to refereed matches only,
    not practice.
  - Entering a refereed fight needs `_vitals_guard`, which refuses when fainted or exhausted, and needs hunger ≥ 10 and
    thirst ≥ 10 (`'too hungry to fight'` / `'too thirsty to fight'`).
- **R7: Shade.**
  - The rings on Bãi đất trống stand under a tin roof, so they count as shade: no heat shock, no rain wetting and no
    lightning mid-fight.
  - The Võ đường's gate is covered by an awning, so it is shade too.
  - `ham_ngam` is shade automatically: 0041's `_in_shade` treats any unlisted map as indoors.
  - 0050 and 0051 re-create `_in_shade` from 0041's body plus their rects. A unit test diffs the bodies.
- **R8: Anti-cheat envelope.** Malformed or impossible input returns the 0015 envelope through `_ac_flag` and never
  raises. The following are answers, never flags:
  - a stale match;
  - pushes after the match ended;
  - a lost claim;
  - a refused challenge.
- **R9: UI.** Vietnamese copy, `.game-ui` parchment panels, `formatXu`. Every new animation has a still fallback under
  reduced motion (no shake, flash or slow-down).

## v20.1 Engine core + arena overlay + practice

### Rulings
- **Entry point.** A punching bag, "Bao cát · Luyện võ", on Chợ Lớn's south pavement.
  - Bag at (1090, 336), use spot (1090, 318) facing down; interactable kind `punch_bag`.
  - E opens the practice overlay. No RPC, no cost, no reward.
  - The bag stays after v20.2 (the Võ đường is built beside it).
- **Style in v20.1.** Everyone fights as **Tự do** (freestyle): the normals, the throw and one special, all at base stats.
  In v20.2 practice also offers every style the player has enrolled in, at their rank, provided they wear its uniform.
- **Practice options.** Bot level 1–5, 1 or 3 rounds, "Bao cát đứng yên" (a dummy that never acts) and a frame-data
  toggle that shows hitboxes (dev aid, allowed in prod since practice pays nothing).

### Engine (`lib/game/fight/engine.ts`)
- **Tick and units.** 60 Hz. Positions are in subpixels (1 px = 256). The stage is the whole canvas and there is no
  camera: fighter centres are clamped to x ∈ [16, 368] px, and the ground is y = 0 with y pointing up.
- **Input mask** (bit, then default key):

  | Bit | Meaning | Key |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | left | A / ← |
  | 1 | right | D / → |
  | 2 | up (jump) | W / ↑ |
  | 3 | down (crouch) | S / ↓ |
  | 4 | LP đấm nhẹ | U |
  | 5 | HP đấm nặng | I |
  | 6 | LK đá nhẹ | J |
  | 7 | HK đá nặng | K |
  | 8 | Đỡ (block) | L |
  | 9 | Chiêu (skill shortcut) | O |

  - Left and right are absolute on the wire. The sim turns them into back and forward from the fighter's facing.
  - Left and right held together count as neutral, as do up and down together.
- **API (pure):**
  - `createMatch(params) → State`
  - `step(state, inP1, inP2) → State`
  - `hash(state) → u32`: FNV-1a over the state's ints, 4 bytes each, little-endian.
  - `runFrames(state, runsP1, runsP2, n)`
  - `State` is a fixed-length `number[]` (about 140 ints). Copying it is the rollback snapshot.
- **Params (`MatchParams`).**
  - `seed` (u32; drives the bots only), `rounds` (1 or 3), `maxRounds` (5).
  - Per fighter: `style`, `rank` (0–4), `movesMask` (the specials unlocked by rank), `hpPct` (100 except bosses) and the
    style stats copied in.
  - `bot` levels for a bot side.
  - The server stores the params on the match, so the replay uses exactly the same numbers.
- **State per fighter (ints):**
  - Position and motion: x, y, vx, vy, facing.
  - Resources: hp, energy.
  - Current action: action id, action frame, move id, hit-connected flag.
  - Stun and knockdown: stun left, stun kind, hitstop, knockdown left, invulnerable-until, armour hits left.
  - Combo taken (hit count, scale): combo hits taken, combo scale.
  - Input history: previous input, buffered button and its frame.
  - Motion memory:
    - `lastDir[1..9]`, the frame each numpad direction was last entered;
    - `prevDir[2,3,6]`, the entry before that;
    - the current direction.
  - Throw-tech window, round wins.
- **Global state (ints):** frame, phase (intro / fight / ko / roundEnd / over), phase frames left, round number, timer
  frames, the PRNG state (mulberry32 = `rand32` from `fishing/reel.ts`), last round's result, match result, and each bot's
  memory (6 ints per bot side).
- **Match flow:**
  - Each round opens with a 90-frame intro ("Hiệp N" … "Đấu!"), during which inputs are ignored.
  - Then comes the fight, with a timer of 5 940 frames (99 s) that runs during hitstop too.
  - A KO or time-up is followed by a 150-frame pause, then the next round.
  - Energy carries over between rounds; HP, positions and the round's state reset.
  - Two round wins win the match.
  - A double KO, or time-up with equal HP‰, is a draw round that gives nobody a win.
  - After round 5 with no winner, the match is a draw.
  - Worst case is 5 × (90 + 5 940 + 150) = 30 900 frames.
- **Motions.** These are recognised from the direction memory, with directions relative to facing in numpad notation
  (6 = forward).

  | Name | Directions | Window |
  |---|---|---|
  | QCF | 2 → 3 → 6 | ≤ 12 frames |
  | QCB | 2 → 1 → 4 | ≤ 12 frames |
  | DP | 6 → 2 → 3 | ≤ 14 frames |
  | ↓↓ | 2, leave, 2 | ≤ 14 frames |
  | QCF×2 | 2 3 6 2 3 6 | ≤ 24 frames |

  - The button press (a rising edge) must come within 6 frames of the motion's last direction.
  - Priority: QCF×2 > DP > QCF > QCB > ↓↓ > normals.
  - A press that cannot act yet is buffered for 4 frames.
- **Chiêu shortcut** (for beginners, keyboard and touch).

  | Input | Special |
  |---|---|
  | O | S1 |
  | O + ← (back) | S2 |
  | O + ↓ | S3 |
  | O + → (forward) | S4 |
  | O + Đỡ | Tuyệt kỹ |

  - A shortcut costs **+100 energy** on top of the special's cost and **+2 startup frames**.
  - A shortcut special with cost 0 still needs 100 energy.
- **Movement:**

  | Movement | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Walk forward | 320 sub/frame (1.25 px) |
  | Walk back | 256 sub/frame |
  | Jump initial vertical speed | 1 792 sub/frame |
  | Jump horizontal speed | ±384 sub/frame |
  | Gravity | 90 sub/frame² |
  | Jump airtime | 40 frames |
  | Jump apex | ≈ 70 px |
  | Pre-jump | 4 frames |
  | Landing | 3 frames |

  - Style walk and jump percentages scale these values, rounding down.
  - Pushboxes are 20 px wide, and the fighters never overlap.
- **Boxes (px, relative to the feet, facing right):**

  | Box | Width | Height |
  |---|---|---|
  | Standing hurtbox | 22 | 60 |
  | Crouching hurtbox | 24 | 38 |
  | Air hurtbox | 20 | 50 |

  Hitboxes come per move (tables below): x-reach, y-low, y-high.
- **Hits:**
  - Hitstop freezes both fighters for 5 frames on a hit and 3 on a block.
  - Hitstun and blockstun count after the hitstop.
  - Combo scaling: the k-th hit of a combo deals `dmg × max(40, 100 − 10·(k−1)) / 100`, integer.
  - Damage is `base × ATK / DEF` of the two styles, in integer per-cent.
  - A hit on an airborne fighter makes it fall, and it is invulnerable until it lands (no juggles).
  - A knockdown lasts 36 frames down plus 20 frames of getup, invulnerable throughout. Knockdowns come from sweeps,
    throws and specials marked KD.
- **Block:**
  - To block, hold back (away from the opponent) or Đỡ while free to act: idle, walking, crouching or already in
    blockstun.
  - Standing blocks highs and mids; crouching (down held) blocks lows and mids.
  - Jump attacks and specials marked OH are overheads, which must be blocked standing.
  - Chip damage is 1/8 on specials and supers and 0 on normals. Chip never KOs; it leaves 1 HP.
- **Throw.**
  - Input: LP + LK pressed within 2 frames of each other, on the ground, within 28 px.
  - Frame data: 5 frames of startup, 2 active and 20 of recovery on a whiff.
  - Damage 110, with a knockdown, and it cannot be blocked.
  - A throw can't grab a fighter in hitstun, blockstun or getup, or one in the air.
  - **Tech:** the defender presses LP + LK within 8 frames of the throw connecting. Both are pushed 40 px apart and nobody
    takes damage.
- **Energy (nội lực, 0–1000):**

  | Event | Energy |
  |---|---|
  | Attacker lands a hit | +50 |
  | Attacker is blocked | +25 |
  | Defender is hit | +30 |
  | Defender blocks | +10 |

  - Each gain is scaled by the style's energy-gain %.
  - Costs:

    | Move | Cost |
    |---|---|
    | S1 | 0 |
    | S2 | 150 |
    | S3 | 200 |
    | S4 | 250 |
    | Tuyệt kỹ | 1000 |

  - A special without enough energy does not come out; the plain button's normal comes out instead.
- **HP:** 1000 × `hpPct`/100 per round.
- **Cancels:** LP, LK, HP and every crouching normal except cr.HK can be cancelled into a special on contact (hit or
  block), from the hitstop through the active frames.

### Bot (`lib/game/fight/bot.ts`, mirrored in SQL by 0049)
- **Decision rhythm.** The bot decides only on decision frames, every *D* frames; this is its reaction time. Between
  decisions it plays out a script of input masks (a motion is a 3–6 frame script). Its rolls use the match's PRNG in
  state, so the replay reproduces it exactly.
- **Inputs to the decision:** distance, whether the foe is airborne, the foe's action phase (startup, active or
  recovery), own HP and energy, and the unlocked specials.
- **Choice.** A weighted pick among approach, retreat, jump-in, poke, block, anti-air, special, throw and super. The
  weights come from the level table:

| Level | D | Block % | Anti-air % | Special % | Tech % | Combo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 24 | 15 | 10 | 10 | 0 | none |
| 2 | 20 | 30 | 25 | 15 | 10 | LP → S1 |
| 3 | 16 | 45 | 40 | 20 | 20 | LP, LP → S1 |
| 4 | 13 | 55 | 55 | 25 | 30 | 2-hit → special |
| 5 | 11 | 62 | 65 | 30 | 40 | as 4, supers at full energy |
| 6 | 9 | 70 | 72 | 35 | 50 | as 5 |
| 7 | 8 | 76 | 80 | 40 | 60 | as 5, punishes whiffed specials |
| 8 | 7 | 82 | 88 | 45 | 70 | as 7 |

### Rendering (`lib/game/fight/render/*`)
- **Canvas.** The arena canvas is **384 × 216** logical pixels (16:9), scaled up by the largest integer that fits (×2 on
  phones, ×3 or ×4 on desktop) with `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- **Ruling: new fighter frames, not a scaled chibi.**
  - The 24 × 48 chibi is two heads tall: at 3× its punches and kicks would read as blobs.
  - The fighter is a **procedural pose rig** in a 48 × 64 box: a 16 × 16 chibi-style head carrying the player's own skin,
    hair style and hair colour (palettes from `look.ts`), and a torso and limbs about 3.5 heads tall.
  - A pose is 14 joints (head, chest, hip, shoulders, elbows, hands, knees, feet) as integer points.
  - The painter rasterises limbs as 3 px (arms) and 4 px (legs) lines with a 1 px outline. It fills them with the
    uniform's colours and draws the belt, armband, sash or waistband in the rank colour. Boxing and Muay Thai get gloves
    or hand wraps.
  - Poses are mirrored for facing left and cached per (look, uniform, rank, pose) in offscreen canvases.
- **Poses.**
  - About 40 shared poses: idle ×4 breathing frames, walk ×4, crouch, jump ×3, block standing and crouching, hit high,
    mid and low, knockdown ×3, getup ×2, throw ×2, victory, defeat, and 2 to 3 keyframes per normal.
  - Each style adds 2 idle frames for its stance (Muay Thai's high guard bounce, Boxing's bob, Karate's kiba-dachi,
    Taekwondo's side bounce, Judo's grip stance, Vịnh Xuân's centre-line hands, Vovinam's trung bình tấn) and 3 to 5
    keyframes per special and super. That is about 35 × 4 more.
- **Poses are a pure function of the state:** `(action, move, action frame)` gives the pose. So a rollback redraws
  correctly with no extra bookkeeping.
- **Effects (render only, never in the sim):**
  - hit sparks: a 4-frame orange star, larger for heavies;
  - block spark: a blue ring;
  - a white flash of 2 frames on the hit fighter;
  - screen shake of 4 frames at ±2 px on heavies, supers and KOs;
  - a super flash: the screen darkens and the style's name banner shows for 30 frames;
  - dust on landings and knockdowns.

  Effects are keyed by (frame, fighter, move), so a rolled-back hit's spark is dropped and a replayed one is not
  doubled.
- **KO screen.**
  - The final hit freezes for 30 rendered frames. This is a render hold: the sim's 150-frame KO pause covers it.
  - Big pixel letters "K.O." appear with "Hạ đo ván!" beneath, and the loser falls.
  - Then comes the round result. At the match's end, a result card shows the winner's victory pose, rounds, stake paid,
    vitals cost and belt or rating changes.
  - "Hết giờ!" is shown at time-up and "Hòa!" for a draw round.
  - Under reduced motion: no shake or flash, the letters appear without the zoom, and the effects become a static
    star.
- **HUD:**
  - Top: the two HP bars (a yellow damage trail catching up after 20 frames), names, style icon and belt colour, round
    pips, and the 99 timer in the centre.
  - Bottom: the energy bars, segmented at 250, with "TK" lit at 1000.
  - A combo counter ("3 đòn!").
  - A network chip (ping, input delay, rollback depth) in PvP.
- **Arenas** (backgrounds 384 × 216, drawn in code, all original):
  - practice: Chợ Lớn street at dusk;
  - dojo: a wood floor, a drum, a banner that reads "Võ đường";
  - Bãi đất trống: packed dirt, a tire stack, a tin-roofed ring, the sky coloured by the room's day or night;
  - the hầm: concrete, a chain-link cage, hanging bulbs, a crowd in silhouette.

### The refereed-match pipeline (built here, reused by v20.3 and v20.4)
- **`fight_matches`** (one row per refereed match):
  - identity: `id uuid pk`, `room_id uuid null` (on delete set null);
  - `kind` in `exam`, `pvp`, `ug_rated`, `ug_ladder`, `ug_cup`;
  - `status` in `live`, `done`, `void`, `disputed`;
  - fighters: `p1 uuid` (red), `p2 uuid null` (null means a bot), `params jsonb` (the full `MatchParams`: seed, the
    input delay, both fighters' style, rank, mask and stats, bot level and HP%);
  - money and place: `stake int default 0`, `ring smallint null`, `ref text` (the exam id, ladder floor or cup id);
  - timing: `started_at` (frame 0 in server time), `ended_at`;
  - replay: `sim int[]`, `sim_frame int default 0`, `sim_hash bigint`;
  - outcome: `rounds jsonb` (per round: winner, reason, HP left, frame), `winner smallint` (1, 2 or 0 for a draw),
    `end_reason` (`ko`, `decision`, `forfeit`, `timeout_claim`, `abandon`, `conflict`, `overtime`);
  - `resyncs smallint`.
- **`fight_logs`**, primary key `(match_id, side)`:
  - `runs int[]`, the canonical own inputs as RLE;
  - `frontier int default -1`, the last frame covered;
  - `seen_runs int[]` and `seen_frontier int`, the opponent's inputs as received (PvP, v20.3);
  - `last_push_at`, `pushes`, `stall_frames` (how long this client waited for the other, as reported), `bad_hashes`.
  - Retention: logs are pruned 14 days after the match ends, lazily; match rows are kept 90 days.
- **`fight_profiles`**, one row per account:
  - `prev_outfit`, `wins`, `losses`, `draws` (refereed PvP only);
  - `day_on`, `staked_today`;
  - `pvp_locked_until` (v20.3).
- **`fight_push(p_session_token, p_match uuid, p_from int, p_runs int[], p_seen_from int default null,
  p_seen_runs int[] default null, p_hash_frame int default null, p_hash bigint default null, p_stall int default 0)
  returns jsonb`.** In order:
  1. Lock the match row. An already-settled match answers its result, and nothing is flagged.
  2. `p_from` must equal `frontier + 1`. A lower value that repeats already-stored frames identically is idempotent (a
     retry). Anything else is hard `fight_bad_input`.
  3. Validate the runs with `_fx_runs_error`, with at most 300 frames per push.
  4. **Pacing:** the new frontier must be ≤ `(now − started_at) × 60 + 60`. Otherwise it is hard `fight_too_fast`: the
     client runs faster than real time, which is how a script would fight a deterministic bot.
  5. Append the runs and update `last_push_at`.
  6. Advance the server sim from `sim_frame` to the covered frontier: the player's frontier for a bot match, the minimum
     of both frontiers in PvP. Store `sim` and `sim_hash`.
  7. If the client's hash at `p_hash_frame` is ≤ `sim_frame` and differs from the server's, soft `fight_hash_mismatch`,
     counted in `bad_hashes`, and the answer carries `resync: true`.
  8. If the sim reached the end of the match, settle it (per kind) in the same transaction.
  9. Answer `{status, sim_frame, frontiers, result?, resync?}`.
- **`fight_state(p_session_token, p_match)`** returns what a client needs to resume after a reload or a resync:
  - the params and `started_at`;
  - its own full runs;
  - the opponent's runs up to `min(frontiers)`;
  - `sim` and `sim_frame`;
  - the status and the result.
- **`fight_forfeit(p_session_token, p_match)`**: "Đầu hàng". The caller loses at once.
- **Bot matches.**
  - `p2` is null and the bot's inputs are computed inside `_fx_step` from the bot memory in state (`_fx_bot`, the mirror
    of `bot.ts`).
  - Frame 0 is `started_at = now() + 3 s`: the "3·2·1" countdown.
  - Leaving the overlay mid-match (or 60 s with no push) is a loss for the player, settled lazily by the next
    `fight_state`, `fight_push` or `dojo_state`.
- **Soft `fight_superhuman`** (evidence only):
  - more than 12 decision-frame reactions of 4 frames or less to the bot's move starts in one match (a block or
    anti-air within 4 frames of startup);
  - or more than 18 mask changes per 60 frames sustained over 600 frames.

### Challenge flow
1. **Taking a corner.** A player at a free corner presses E: `ring_take(room, token, ring, corner)`.
   - It requires a uniform of an enrolled style, and `_vitals_guard` with hunger and thirst ≥ 10.
   - It refuses if the anti-cheat lock is on, if `pvp_locked_until` is in the future, or if the player is already in a
     corner or a live match.
   - The corner is held for 5 minutes without an opponent, then released lazily.
   - The map channel gets a hint `rg {ring, v}` (budget 2/s, burst 4). Clients on `bai_dat` fetch `ring_state` 150 ms
     later, and poll every 5 s while any ring they can see is occupied.
2. **The ready screen** opens for both once the ring has a red and a blue fighter. It shows:
   - both fighters' rig previews in their uniforms;
   - style, belt and PvP record;
   - the ring's stake;
   - the network line "Ping 84 ms · Trễ 4 khung";
   - the buttons "Đồng ý", "Đổi mức cược" and "Rời sàn".
   - Both clients join the topic `fight:{roomId}:r{ring}` and exchange 10 ping/pongs (`fp`/`fq`, 2 per second) to
     measure the RTT p90.
3. **Stakes.** The presets are **0 (giao hữu), 100, 500, 1 000, 2 000, 5 000, 10 000** xu.
   - `ring_offer(ring, stake, n)` sets the offer and bumps its version. The proposer counts as accepting it.
   - The other player must call `ring_accept(ring, v, n)` with the same version. Any new offer resets both acceptances.
   - An offer lapses after 30 s.
   - `n` is the input delay each client proposes from its own measurement (below). The match uses the larger of the
     two.
4. **Both accepted** means the server, in one transaction:
   - locks both wallets in account-id order;
   - re-checks the uniforms, the vitals, the caps (below) and both balances ≥ stake. A shortfall answers
     `'not enough xu'` naming who, and nothing moves;
   - debits the stake from each (`fight_stake`);
   - creates the `fight_matches` row (kind `pvp`, 3 rounds, params) with `started_at = now() + 3 s`;
   - clears the offer.
   - Both clients get the match id, the params and `started_at`. The "3·2·1" countdown starts, then the overlay on the
     Bãi đất arena.
5. **The end.** The result card shows the rounds, "+1 000 xu" or "−1 000 xu", the vitals cost and the record. The
   buttons are "Tái đấu" (a new offer at the same stake) and "Rời sàn". Both stay in their corners.

- **Caps** (in `fight_config`, editable by an admin RPC):
  - at most **2 live PvP matches per music room** and **4 in the whole project** (the realtime budget below);
  - at most **15 staked matches per account per VN day**;
  - at most **3 staked matches per pair of accounts per VN day**;
  - friendly (stake 0) matches: at most 10 per pair per day.
  - Refusals are `'ring busy'` and `'daily fight limit'`. They are answers, never flags.

### Netcode: ruling **rollback (GGPO-style) with a small input delay**
**Why rollback, not delay-based lockstep:**
- Inputs travel through Supabase's Broadcast relay, not peer to peer: about 40–80 ms one way inside Vietnam and
  Singapore.
- The free plan's message quota (below) forces batching: one packet every 6 frames.
- So the opponent's confirmed input typically arrives **8–12 frames late**.
- Pure lockstep would have to delay every local button by that much (130–200 ms), which makes blocking and anti-airs
  feel broken.
- Rollback keeps the local controls at 2–6 frames and hides the rest by predicting.
- The engine makes rollback cheap:
  - The state is about 140 ints; a snapshot is an array copy.
  - Resimulating 12 frames of two fighters costs well under 1 ms.
  - Determinism is required for the server replay anyway.
  - Poses and effects are pure functions of state (v20.1).
- Under a bad link rollback degrades into stalls, which is lockstep's behaviour: the fallback comes for free.
- The cost is visible corrections ("pops") when a prediction was wrong. The money-relevant moments are never predicted:
  KO, round and match banners and the result card are drawn only from **confirmed** frames.

**The numbers:**
- **Packets.**
  - A packet goes every **6 frames** (10 Hz), or early when an attack button's press edge occurs, provided at least 3
    frames have passed since the last send.
  - At most 12 a second per player. Budget `fi`: 15 per second, burst 20, per sender.
  - A packet `fi = {f, r, a, h?}`:
    - `f`, the first frame;
    - `r`, the RLE runs of own inputs for every frame from the opponent's last ack + 1 to the newest;
    - `a`, the highest frame of the opponent's inputs received contiguously;
    - `h`, `[frame, hash]` for the newest frame confirmed on both sides, every 60 frames.
  - Resending everything not acknowledged covers messages lost during a websocket reconnect.
- **Lag L.** L is the expected lag of the opponent's confirmed input in frames:
  `L = ceil((rttP90/2 + 100 ms) / 16.67)`.
  - The 100 ms is the worst-case batching.
  - rttP90 is measured on the ready screen and re-measured from the packets' acks during play.
- **Input delay N.** `N = clamp(ceil(L / 2), 2, 6)`.
  - It is fixed for the match; the server stores the larger of the two proposals in the params.
  - A match with L > 18 (≈ 300 ms) is refused on the ready screen: "Mạng hai bên chậm quá để đấu (≈ 320 ms)".
  - Examples: an 80 ms RTT gives L = 9, N = 5 and rollbacks of about 4 frames; a 160 ms RTT gives L = 11, N = 6 and
    rollbacks of about 5 frames.
- **Rollback window W = 12 frames.**
  - If the newest confirmed opponent frame is more than W behind the local frame, the local sim **stalls**. After 3 s
    it shows "⏳ Đang chờ đối thủ…".
  - Predicting means repeating the opponent's last confirmed mask.
- **Time sync.** A client whose local frame runs more than 2 frames ahead of its estimate of the opponent's skips 1 frame
  in every 20 until they are level. This is the GGPO method, and it keeps rollbacks symmetric.
- **The clock.**
  - Frame 0 is at `started_at` in server time.
  - Each client estimates its offset from the server's `now()` in RPC answers: the median of the last 5
    (answer − round-trip/2).
- **Pushes.**
  - Each client calls `fight_push` every 60 frames with its own confirmed runs, `seen` (the opponent's inputs as
    received, for the same range), its latest confirmed hash and its stall frames.
  - During a stall it still pushes an empty keepalive every 3 s.
- **Spectating** (v20.4 only) uses confirmed frames with a 30-frame buffer and never rolls back.

**Desync, resync and reload (procedure R).**
- **Triggers:**
  - the peers' `h` hashes differ for the same frame;
  - a push answers `resync: true`;
  - a page reload in mid-match: on load `fight_state` finds a live match and reopens the overlay.
- **What happens:**
  1. The client calls `fight_state`.
  2. It sets its sim to the server's `sim` at `sim_frame`, which is the canonical `min(frontiers)`.
  3. It re-applies both sides' canonical inputs beyond that frame: its own pushed runs are final, and the opponent's
     come from `fight_state` and broadcast.
  4. It continues.
- The opponent is told with `fr {frame}` and does the same.
- **Pushed inputs are final.** A client never re-plays different inputs for frames it has pushed.
- A match counts its resyncs. A client's hash that disagrees with the server's own replay is soft
  `fight_hash_mismatch`: the sim was modified. It becomes a hard flag at 3 such matches in 7 days.
- Honest clients only desync through an engine bug, and the server's replay is the referee either way.

### Settlement and the forfeit rules
- **Normal end.** The push that brings the server sim to the match's end settles it:
  - the winner gets **2 × stake** (`fight_win`);
  - a draw refunds both (`fight_refund`);
  - the records are updated (`wins`, `losses`, `draws`);
  - each player pays the vitals cost (R6);
  - a news item goes to Tin làng for a stake ≥ 5 000, in 18.11's template ("Trận thư hùng ở Bãi đất trống…").
- **Surrender** (`fight_forfeit`): the surrenderer loses the match at once, with the stake to the opponent.
- **Absence (rage quit, a closed tab, a lost connection).**
  - A player whose `last_push_at` is older than **20 s** while the opponent pushed within the last 10 s can be claimed
    with **`fight_claim(token, match)`**.
  - Claiming settles the match as the absentee's loss (`timeout_claim`), whatever the score.
  - The claim button shows on the waiting overlay after 20 s: "Đối thủ mất kết nối · Xử thắng".
  - A reconnect within the 20 s resumes through procedure R.
- **Both absent 120 s:** the match is **voided**. Both are refunded, and soft `fight_abandon` goes to both. This is
  applied lazily by any `ring_*`, `fight_*` or `ring_state` call in the room.
- **Overtime cap 20 min** (stalls included), applied lazily:
  1. The server replays to `min(frontiers)`.
  2. The current round goes to the higher HP‰; equal HP‰ is a draw round.
  3. The match is decided by rounds so far; a tie is a draw with refunds (`overtime`).
- **Leaving the map, a faint, exhaustion or game mode closing** is absence: the same claim rule applies.
- **Account wipe or deletion:** a live match is settled first as that account's forfeit, so the opponent is paid before
  the wallet is cleared, as v16 does.

### Log conflicts (a player's broadcast differing from their push)
- **Detection.** Each push's `seen` is compared with the opponent's canonical runs over the frames both cover. Parts
  still pending are compared when the other side pushes.
  - Honest clients never differ: the client pushes exactly the runs it broadcast.
- **The first mismatch:**
  - halts the match (`status = 'disputed'`), and the next push or poll shows "Trận đấu bị hủy do dữ liệu hai bên không
    khớp";
  - **voids** it with refunds to both;
  - writes a `fight_conflicts` row (the match, both claims for the range, who reported and who was reported);
  - soft-flags `fight_log_conflict` to both.
- **Pattern rule.**
  - An account involved in **2 conflicts in 30 days** gets `pvp_locked_until = now() + 7 days` for staked PvP only:
    "Tạm khóa đấu cược 7 ngày". Friendly fights, practice and exams stay open.
  - **3 conflicts with at least 2 distinct opponents** is hard `fight_log_conflict_pattern`.
- **Why void, not settle.**
  - With an untrusted relay the server cannot tell who lied: A pushing Y while broadcasting X, or B reporting a false
    `seen`.
  - A void gains a losing cheater at most one escape before the lock, and the evidence goes to the admin tab.
- **Rejected: routing inputs through RPCs** so the server relays them. That would mean 20–40 PostgREST calls a second per
  fight, and the latency would kill the feel.
- **Lookahead** (withholding own inputs until the opponent's arrive): the opponent sees a stall.
  - Each client reports the stall frames it waited for the other.
  - Soft `fight_stall_blame` when one side caused more than 20 % of a match's frames of stall, in 3 or more matches
    against at least 2 opponents in 7 days.

### Realtime budget (v20.3)
- **Traffic per fight:** about 12 `fi` a second per player, plus 2 pings a second on the ready screen. That is about 24
  sends a second and, counting deliveries, about **50 metered messages a second**: about 170 000 per fight-hour.
- **Against the free plan:**
  - The free plan's 100 messages a second allows about 2 live fights beside the existing map traffic, hence the cap of 2
    per room and 4 per project.
  - Its 2 M messages a month cover **about 11 fight-hours a month**.
  - The Pro plan's quota covers about 30 (Open question 1).
- The ring hint `rg` adds under 1 message a minute per ring.
- **Transport seam.** `FightTransport` has `send(packet)`, `onPacket` and `close`, so a WebRTC data channel can replace
  Broadcast later with no engine change.
